# Personal voice (user's own writing)

Reference sample: "If Only I Had the Courage" by the user,
https://dir.thisnot.my.id/w/if-only-i-had-the-courage (used as the style
reference at the user's request). The user also likes the plain explanatory
voice used in the first draft, so treat this as an **option**, not a
replacement: default stays explanatory, personal voice is applied when the
user asks for it or points at a personal sample.

## Distilled rules

- First person, confessional. Present-tense recollection of the past.
- Very short paragraphs. Often one sentence per paragraph.
- Short sentences, few subordinate clauses. Concrete nouns, no abstraction.
- Em dashes for turns and asides.
- Dialogue and verbatim reported speech in curly quotes.
- Emphasis by capitalising a whole line: "I WILL NEVER BE ABLE TO BE WITH HER."
- Ends on a single-line kicker.
- No headings inside the piece; no throat-clearing introduction.
- Regret and longing stated flatly, not decorated.

## How to mix it with a sourced post

- Personal voice owns the frame (anecdote) and the coda. The explanatory
  middle stays plain so the citations do not fight the prose.
- Citations still attach to every factual claim. A personal voice never
  exempts a statistic from a `[n]`.
- Do not copy the source site's typographic quirks (a space before
  punctuation, e.g. "Her ."). That is the site's rendering, not the intent.
- Do not let the kicker replace a sourced conclusion.
